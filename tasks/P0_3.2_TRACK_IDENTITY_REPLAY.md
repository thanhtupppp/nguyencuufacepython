# P0.3.2 — Track identity replay and multi-face safety

## Goal

Validate that camera-local `track_id` remains separate from global `person_id` and that temporal confirmation does not cause identity switches during missed detections, short occlusions, crossing faces, or conflicting recognition candidates.

## Required protocol

Replay deterministic JSONL observations. Each observation contains `frame_index`, `track_id`, `person_id`, `accepted`, and optional `score`. The evaluator must measure identity switches and confirmation latency per track.

Scenarios:

1. single face: same person across consecutive frames;
2. two faces crossing: A/B/A/B track observations with stable track IDs;
3. one-frame miss followed by recovery;
4. short occlusion within `max_gap_frames`;
5. long disappearance beyond `max_gap_frames`;
6. conflicting candidate while a track is already confirmed;
7. simultaneous tracks with different person IDs.

## Acceptance

- replay is deterministic;
- `track_id` and `person_id` never share a namespace;
- a new candidate needs `min_consecutive` accepted observations before confirmation;
- conflicting identity cannot silently replace an already confirmed identity;
- long disappearance transitions to `LOST`;
- identity-switch count is reported explicitly;
- confirmation latency is reported in frames;
- no recognition threshold is changed to hide tracking errors.
