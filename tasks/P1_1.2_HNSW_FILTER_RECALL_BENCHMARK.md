# P1.1.2 — HNSW filtered person-level recall benchmark

## Status
IMPLEMENTED_HARNESS — execution receipt pending real PostgreSQL/pgvector.

## Goal
Measure ANN recall under the exact person-level filtering conditions used by recognition. Do not change recognition thresholds to compensate for retrieval misses.

## Scope
Compare the correctness-first person-level query with a candidate-first HNSW query. Record `hnsw.ef_search` 40/80/160/320 and `hnsw.iterative_scan` off/strict_order/relaxed_order where supported. Include 100/1k/10k people, 1/3/10/30 templates, 50/90% inactive selectivity, and model-version selectivity.

## Verification
Against a locked exact person-level reference, report recall@1/@5/@10, missing-result rate, duplicate person_id rate, p50/p95/p99 latency, PostgreSQL/pgvector version, index/table size, and `EXPLAIN (ANALYZE, BUFFERS)` plans. Candidate-first must overfetch candidates and deduplicate by person_id before final LIMIT.

## Acceptance
- exact reference remains the correctness oracle;
- no duplicate person_id;
- all active/model filters are enforced;
- recall loss is visible and blocks production acceptance;
- latency improvements do not justify unsafe recall loss;
- no recognition threshold or margin changes are used to hide retrieval misses.

## Architecture rule
Do not replace the current person-level search, introduce one-vector-per-person, or change schema/index strategy until recall, safety, and latency gates are met on the real deployment.

## Next
