# P0.3 — Temporal tracking and multi-frame confirmation

Status: READY-NEXT after recognition quality calibration.

## Objective
Reduce frame-to-frame identity flicker and prevent a single noisy recognition from becoming a person_id event. Tracking is an association layer, not an identity decision.

## Baseline
Start with a lightweight IoU + Kalman-style tracker or ByteTrack-compatible association over SCRFD boxes. Do not require a second neural tracker model until benchmark evidence shows it is needed.

ByteTrack is the challenger because it associates lower-confidence detections and is deployable with ONNX/CPU-oriented pipelines, but its MOT benchmark numbers must not be treated as face-specific results.

## Identity contract
- `track_id` is local to one camera stream and is never a `person_id`.
- Recognition attaches a candidate `person_id` to a track only after temporal confirmation.
- Keep `unknown` as a valid state.
- Never propagate a confirmed person_id across cameras without an explicit cross-camera identity event.

## Verification
Measure IDF1/HOTA or equivalent track association metrics where a labeled video set exists, plus project-specific:
- identity switches;
- false person_id attachments;
- unknown-to-known transition errors;
- confirmation latency in frames;
- recognition calls per second with and without tracking;
- p50/p95 end-to-end latency.

Test occlusion, missed detections, crossing faces, entry/exit, blur, and multiple simultaneous faces.

## Completion criteria
- stable track_id lifecycle and expiry rules;
- temporal confirmation state machine;
- no track_id/person_id namespace collision;
- measured reduction in false identity switches without unacceptable confirmation delay;
- deterministic replay test fixture.
