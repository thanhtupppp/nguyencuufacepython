# P0.4.1.1.4 — Deterministic SCRFD → alignment regression fixture

Status: PARTIAL_COMPLETE

## Objective

Verify that the production face crop is deterministic and that the same canonical aligned crop can be consumed by both ArcFace and the anti-spoof benchmark.

## Completed in this gate

- Deterministic synthetic BGR fixture with no external binary dependency.
- Exact 112x112 output shape and uint8 contract.
- Repeated alignment produces byte-identical output.
- SHA-256 regression hash is pinned in the test.
- Known scale/translation landmarks are accepted by the similarity-transform baseline.

- Verify BGR/RGB channel handling at the shared-crop boundary.

## Verification
