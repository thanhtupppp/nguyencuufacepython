# P1.1.1 — Vector recall benchmark harness

## Status
IMPLEMENTED_HARNESS — runtime benchmark pending PostgreSQL/pgvector execution.

## Goal
Provide a reproducible, correctness-first measurement harness for person-level vector retrieval before any ANN/schema architecture change.

## Verification
`tools/benchmark_vector_recall.py` creates an isolated benchmark schema, deterministic normalized 512D gallery/query data, computes an exact person-level reference, and compares it with the current person-level SQL. It reports recall@1/@5/@10, missing-result rate, duplicate person IDs, and p50/p95/p99 latency plus PostgreSQL/pgvector versions.

The harness refuses to produce a project benchmark without `DATABASE_URL` and does not tune recognition thresholds.

## Scenarios
- 100 people × 1 template
- 100 people × 10 templates
- 1,000 people × 10 templates
- 10,000 people × 10 templates
- mixed 1/3/10/30 templates per person
- 50% and 90% inactive-person selectivity

## Acceptance
- exact reference is the correctness gate;
- no duplicate `person_id` in returned candidates;
- no silent person-level false negatives accepted;
- model-version and active-person filters remain enforced;
- recall loss is reported, never hidden by threshold changes;
- production architecture is unchanged until recall, safety, and latency gates pass.

## Next
Run the harness against the real PostgreSQL/pgvector deployment, then add HNSW candidate variants (`ef_search` 40/80/160/320 and iterative_scan where supported), followed by candidate-first and one-vector-per-person prototypes.
