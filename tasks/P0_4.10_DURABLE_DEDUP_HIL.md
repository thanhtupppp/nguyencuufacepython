# P0.4.10 — Durable deduplication + hardware-in-the-loop

## Status
IMPLEMENTED — execution/HIL receipt pending

## Objective
Make authorization event delivery restart-safe and define the boundary between durable event deduplication and physical actuator semantics.

## Implementation
- `src/events/durable_dedup.py`: SQLite event ledger with CLAIMED/COMPLETED states.
- `tests/test_durable_action_ledger.py`: restart and duplicate-claim coverage.

## Safety contract
A durable ledger prevents a previously completed `event_id` from being accepted again after restart. It does **not** make an arbitrary physical side effect exactly-once across a crash between CLAIMED and actuator completion.

For production, the actuator/device must accept `event_id` as an idempotency key. HIL must explicitly test power loss/reconnect at the claim/action/complete boundaries.

## HIL acceptance
1. same `event_id` delivered N times => at most one physical action;
2. restart after COMPLETED => duplicate is rejected;
3. reconnect/duplicate MQTT QoS 1 delivery => no second action;
4. crash-window behavior is observable and recoverable, not silently claimed exactly-once;
5. receipt records event_id, device_id, status, and timestamps.
